# Personal Knowledge Boundary

The distributable SIMS Article Creator package must not contain real tenant/blog names, tenant URLs, owner-specific article IDs, or owner-only operational history.

Allowed:
- synthetic examples such as `サンプルブログ`, `example.invalid`, and `A900xxx` / `A999999`;
- generalized editorial knowledge, contracts, patterns, and regression fixtures.

Tenant-specific context belongs in current Evidence or external Personal Editorial Knowledge.
