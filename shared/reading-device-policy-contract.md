# Reading Device Policy Contract

Canonical field: `reading_device_policy` (string). Accepted values: `MOBILE_FIRST`, `DESKTOP_FIRST`, `BALANCED`; default and unknown-value fallback: `BALANCED`.

This is an **advisory editorial preference**, not a new Case status, a ranking factor, or a required field in existing article output schemas. SBM owns the site setting and change history. The consuming product uses `knowledge/reading-device-editorial-policy.md` for editorial behavior.

Never initiate a bulk rewrite, monitoring reset, or automatic publication because of a policy change. Preserve existing approval gates, evidence requirements and the product-specific responsibility boundary.
